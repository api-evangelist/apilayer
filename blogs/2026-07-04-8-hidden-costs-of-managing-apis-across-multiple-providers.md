This article breaks down eight hidden operational costs of managing APIs from multiple vendors, from billing complexity and credential sprawl to fragmented support and compliance overhead. It estimates managing eight API providers costs roughly 16 hours a month in admin work, about 24 working days a year, and argues API sprawl worsens when teams add tools faster than they add ownership.
