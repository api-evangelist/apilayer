This comparison guide recommends Weatherstack, starting at $9.99/month for 50,000 calls, for standard apps needing current and historical weather data, and OpenWeatherMap for projects requiring minute-level precipitation forecasts, radar visualizations, or specialized workflows. The verdict: Weatherstack fits when weather data supports the product, OpenWeatherMap when weather data is the product.
